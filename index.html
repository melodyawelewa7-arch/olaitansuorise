Frequently asked questions

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Use the custom surprise form and tell us your budget, occasion and what you have in mind.

How do I place an order?

Add your preferred package to the cart, enter the recipient and delivery details, then send the order through WhatsApp for confirmation.

Can I add a personal message?

Absolutely. You can include a gift note or special instruction with your order.

How much is delivery?

Delivery charges depend on the destination and order. We confirm the delivery fee before final payment.

Do you accept urgent orders?

Urgent or same-day requests may be possible depending on location, stock and preparation time. Contact us first.

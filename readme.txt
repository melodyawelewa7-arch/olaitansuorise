BEFORE LAUNCH — REQUIRED
1. Open script.js.
2. Find:
   const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "2340000000000";
3. Replace it with the real business WhatsApp number.
   Example: const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "2348012345678";
   Use international format, no +, spaces or leading 0.
4. Replace the sample gallery tiles with real product photos if available.
5. Update package prices to the company's real prices.
6. Replace the email address in index.html if the business has a real email.
7. Add real social-media links.
8. Update delivery wording/areas to match the business.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS SETUP
This version uses WhatsApp to confirm orders. It does NOT pretend that payment has been completed.
The customer sends the order, and the business confirms stock, delivery fee and final payment details.
For online card/bank payments, a payment provider and secure backend should be connected separately.

HOW TO TEST
Open index.html in Chrome. Add packages to the cart, enter test customer details and click "Send Order on WhatsApp" after adding the real number.

HOSTING
This is a static site. You can upload index.html, style.css and script.js to a static host such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.
